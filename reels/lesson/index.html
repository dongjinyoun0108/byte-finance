How super
actually works
Your employer chips in
Employers pay a share of your ordinary earnings into a super fund in your name, on top of your pay.
Locked away for later
You generally can’t access it until you reach preservation age and retire, with a few exceptions.
Your fund puts it to work
Funds spread it across assets through options like balanced or high growth. Fees and returns differ between funds.
Taxed lightly
That’s generally lower than most people’s income tax rate. Higher earners can pay more.
