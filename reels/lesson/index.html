Interest on interest
You earn interest on your savings, then interest on that interest. Growth speeds up the longer it runs.
After 10 years
Compounded yearly at 5%, $10,000 grows to about $16,289 in a decade.
Time does the work
Double the time and the result is far more than double the first decade’s gain.
It cuts both ways
Credit card debt compounds the same way, which is why unpaid balances can snowball.
